About

How the spreadsheet works: each Battlepass level costs a set number of documents, split across types like Financial, Medical, or PMC files. The total for a level is the same for everyone, but which types make it up is personal to you — that's why there's no pre-filled spreadsheet. Fill in your own per-type counts for each level (Excel or CSV, your choice), keeping each row's document-type columns adding up to its Total Documents value. Then use Import spreadsheet below to load those costs into the app. Claiming and unclaiming rewards happens here in the app — the spreadsheet only ever defines what each level costs, not your progress.

Claim Mode
What is this?

When on, claiming any reward skips the document-cost check entirely — nothing is required or deducted. An override for corrections, not normal play.

Unclaim Mode
What is this?

When off, the Unclaim button doesn't show up on claimed rewards at all, so a claim can't be undone by accident. Turn this on to unclaim something (and refund its documents, unless it was claimed via Claim Mode).

Each level has its own standalone document cost (not cumulative with other levels), shared across all game modes. Editing battlepass.xlsx (or .csv) directly and re-importing is usually easier than hand-editing here.

About

How the spreadsheet works: each Battlepass level costs a set number of documents, split across types like Financial, Medical, or PMC files. The total for a level is the same for everyone, but which types make it up is personal to you — that's why there's no pre-filled spreadsheet. Fill in your own per-type counts for each level (Excel or CSV, your choice), keeping each row's document-type columns adding up to its Total Documents value. Then use Read from spreadsheet below to load those costs into the app. Claiming and unclaiming rewards happens here in the app — the spreadsheet only ever defines what each level costs, not your progress.

New to the app? Take a guided tour of the main screen.
Claim Mode
What is this?

When on, claiming any reward skips the document-cost check entirely — nothing is required or deducted. An override for corrections, not normal play.

Unclaim Mode
What is this?

When off, the Unclaim button doesn't show up on claimed rewards at all, so a claim can't be undone by accident. Turn this on to unclaim something (and refund its documents, unless it was claimed via Claim Mode).

Your progress is backed up automatically once a day, in case you ever need to undo a mistake.

Welcome

Every reward, page, and document total in this season is already built in — it's the same for everyone, so there's nothing to set up there.

The one thing that's actually personal to you is which document types make up each level's cost — that varies player to player even though the total doesn't. That's why every Claim button starts greyed out: nothing's been filled in yet.

Fastest way to fix that: open Settings and fill in your numbers in the Levels section. A spreadsheet is there too, if you'd rather work that way or want a backup.
